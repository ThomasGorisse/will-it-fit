Will that sofa fit in your living room? Can the fridge get through the door? Will It Fit helps you picture the space furniture takes up, at home or before moving day.

START WITH THE SIZE
Pick a 2-seat or 3-seat sofa, a bed for a 140 or 160 cm mattress, a wardrobe, fridge, washer, 55-inch or 65-inch TV, bike or stroller. Each comes with typical dimensions already entered. Or type your own width, depth and height in centimeters or inches.

SEE IT IN A 3D ROOM
A live 3D room shows a simple model of your object at the size you entered, with dimension lines on the floor. Change a measurement and watch the model resize. Rotate the view to get a feel for its proportions.

TRY IT IN YOUR ROOM WITH AR
Point your phone at the floor and let the object settle into place at real size using augmented reality. Move it and turn it to try different spots. Its scale stays locked to your dimensions.

Take a photo with the dimensions written on it, then share it with a housemate, a friend or whoever is helping you move.

MEASURE WITH AR
Use the AR tape to mark two points on a surface. Apply the measured distance to the object's width, depth or height, or to the door's width or height.

WILL IT GO THROUGH THE DOOR?
Enter the clear width and height inside the door frame. Get a verdict, see how much room is left or how much too big the object is, and watch the suggested carrying orientation in 3D.

The door check compares six orientations for a straight passage, without diagonal tilting. Allow space for your hands and for maneuvering on either side.

WILL IT MAKE THE TURN IN THE HALLWAY?
Enter the hallway widths before and after the turn and the ceiling height. Get a verdict for a right-angle turn with the object carried level.

WILL IT FIT IN THE TRUNK (CAR BOOT)?
Enter the opening width and height and the usable length, width and height inside. The check compares six orientations for straight loading, without diagonal tilting.

SHARE A CHECK AND REUSE SIZES
Share a door, hallway or car boot check with a link that reopens it in the installed app, with the same object and space dimensions.

Reuse up to three recent custom sizes without typing them again. On supported launchers, long-press the app icon to open shortcuts to those sizes.

FREE AND OFFLINE
Free, no ads, no account. The app works offline. The 3D room and all three fit checks work without AR support.

CHECK THE REAL MEASUREMENTS
Typical sizes are a starting point, not the dimensions of a particular product. Check the seller's numbers, including frames, feet and handles, and enter your actual measurements.

AR is a preview, and the AR tape can be a few centimeters off. For a tight fit, confirm with a tape measure. AR needs a compatible phone and Google Play Services for AR, which may need to be installed first. A well-lit floor with visible texture helps placement.
